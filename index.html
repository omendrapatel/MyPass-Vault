Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MyPass Vault offline security model.

Is MyPass Vault truly 100% offline without internet?

Yes! MyPass Vault does not connect to any remote server, cloud API, or third-party database. All passwords, 2FA tokens, and diary logs are encrypted locally on your physical hardware using AES-256 bit encryption.

How does the 2FA / TOTP Authenticator generate codes offline?

The RFC 6238 TOTP algorithm uses the standard mathematical combination of your secret key and the local system clock timestamp. Because time is calculated locally by your CPU, no internet or cellular network connection is needed.

How does the 30-Day Trash Bin work?

When you delete a password or diary note, it is moved to the centralized Trash bin instead of being permanently lost immediately. You can restore it at any time. Any item remaining in Trash for over 30 days is automatically purged permanently.

What is the Decoy / Duress PIN (Fake Vault)?

In settings, you can configure a secondary Decoy MPIN. If someone physically forces you to unlock your vault, entering the Decoy PIN opens an empty fake vault, ensuring your real confidential passwords remain completely invisible and protected.

How can I backup or transfer data between PC and Android?

You can export your database anytime into a password-protected .mypass file from Settings. You can transfer this encrypted file via USB, local Wi-Fi, or pendrive and import it into either the Windows Desktop app or Android app.
